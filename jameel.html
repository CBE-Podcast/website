JAMEEL MAYERS.

BIG QUESTIONS. QUICK REACTIONS. VERY LITTLE PATIENCE FOR NONSENSE.

Jameel Mayers is one half of the conversation behind Certified Bad Example. With August Black, he digs into the viral stories that make you stop scrolling and ask: who thought this was a good idea?

His perspective brings together creative curiosity, fatherhood, sharp observations, and a willingness to challenge the easy answer. From parenting and public accountability to internet culture and spectacular lapses in judgment, Jameel wants to know what happened, why it happened, and what we can learn without pretending everyone has it figured out.

The result is an unscripted back-and-forth: humor, disagreement, occasional disbelief, and a lot of questions worth asking.
