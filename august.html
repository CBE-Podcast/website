AWARD-WINNING VOICE ACTOR · CO-HOST

AUGUST BLACK.

A VOICE WORTH HEARING. AN OPINION YOU WON’T FORGET.

August Black is an award-winning voice actor, creative storyteller, and co-host of Certified Bad Example. Professionally, he’s made a career of bringing stories and characters to life. Behind the podcast microphone, he brings that same expressive energy, sharp humor, and unapologetic perspective to the questionable decisions making headlines.

Alongside Jameel Mayers, August tackles viral stories, parenting dilemmas, pop culture, and those moments when common sense seems to have left the room. His perspective as a father adds a personal dimension to the conversations, without turning the show into a lecture.

Whether he’s breaking down a bad decision or celebrating someone getting it right, August is here for the conversation—not a scripted answer.
